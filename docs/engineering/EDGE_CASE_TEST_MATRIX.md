# SplitFlow Edge-Case Test Matrix

This matrix answers the reviewer question: what messy cases does SplitFlow handle, and which invariants keep the workflow safe?

| Case | Example messy prompt | Expected interpretation | Required deterministic behavior | Safety invariant | Current support status | Related test file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equal split | "Split 100,000 won between 5 people." | One shared item, five active participants. | Each active participant gets a deterministic equal share; shares sum to total. | Share total equals expense total. | Supported | `tests/unit/expense-parser.test.ts`, `tests/unit/domain.test.ts` |
| Unequal shares | "Aina pays 50,000 fixed and the rest split the remainder." | Fixed contribution plus flexible remainder. | Fixed amount is honored and the rest is distributed deterministically. | Final shares are generated by code. | Partially supported: domain supports fixed/weighted/percentage; interactive parser coverage is narrower. | `tests/unit/domain.test.ts` |
| Someone only had drinks | "Mina only had drinks." | Mina should be eligible for drinks, not food items. | Item-level eligibility must exclude Mina from non-drink items. | Eligibility is deterministic, not AI-owned. | Partially supported: item eligibility exists; parser handles simple exclusions better than broad "only had X" allocation. | `tests/unit/itemized-split-engine.test.ts`, `tests/unit/expense-parser.test.ts` |
| Someone opts out of an item | "Daniel does not eat beef, so do not charge him for meat." | Daniel excluded from meat only. | Meat share is split among eligible participants; Daniel remains included in other items. | Per-item eligibility and share reconciliation. | Supported | `tests/unit/itemized-split-engine.test.ts`, `tests/unit/prototype-proposals.test.ts` |
| One person paid separately | "Ali paid 30,000 for drinks and I paid the rest." | Multiple payers in the same proposal. | Paid-by totals and net balances reconcile; settlement instructions reimburse the correct payer. | Paid totals equal cost total; net balances sum to zero. | Supported for modeled payer records; no bank verification. | `tests/unit/itemized-split-engine.test.ts`, `tests/unit/expense-parser.test.ts` |
| Tax/service charge included | "The total was 128,000 including service charge." | Service charge is included in total or modeled as a cost item. | Included amount is allocated like any other eligible item. | Calculation total must match proposal total. | Partially supported: works when represented as an item or total; no tax-specific policy engine yet. | `tests/unit/expense-parser.test.ts` |
| Discount applied | "We had a 10,000 discount." | Discount should reduce the allocatable total or apply to specific items. | Negative or discount allocations need explicit deterministic policy. | No hidden money adjustments. | Not yet supported as a first-class discount rule. | None |
| Rounding remainder | "Split 10,000 KRW across 3 people." | One participant receives the extra 1 KRW by deterministic order. | Remainder assignment is stable and auditable. | Rounding is deterministic and auditable. | Supported | `tests/unit/domain.test.ts`, `tests/unit/itemized-split-engine.test.ts`, `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |
| Participant changes their claim after proposal | "Sarah first claimed 10,000, then says it was 12,000." | Payment evidence is unresolved and needs organizer review. | Conflicting claims cannot be treated as confirmed payment. | Conflicting unresolved claims block readiness. | Partially supported: invariant detects conflicts; production proof/audit workflow is not built. | `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |
| Missing participant confirmation | "Everyone except Ali confirmed." | Ali remains pending. | Proposal remains not ready; next action is reminder/review. | Active participants must accept before readiness. | Supported | `tests/unit/readiness.test.ts`, `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |
| Conflicting claims | "Sarah says she paid 10,000; organizer record says disputed 12,000." | Same participant relationship has contradictory unresolved records. | Mark as needs review and prevent ready-to-pay. | Conflicting claims block readiness. | Partially supported: deterministic detection exists; UI resolution is still prototype-level. | `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |
| Proposal recalculation after opt-out | "Minji is out." | Minji is excluded; remaining shares may change. | Recalculate deterministic shares and require reconfirmation. | Recalculation invalidates prior acceptance. | Supported in workflow/prototype paths. | `tests/unit/split.test.ts`, `tests/unit/workflow-repository.test.ts`, `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |
| Ready-to-pay blocked by unresolved claims | "Sarah already sent me 10,000, but I need to confirm it." | Sarah's payment is a claim, not confirmed money. | Proposal cannot be ready until organizer confirms, disputes, or voids the claim. | Unresolved claims block readiness. | Supported | `tests/unit/readiness.test.ts`, `tests/unit/financial-invariants.test.ts` |

## What This Matrix Does Not Claim

This prototype does not yet prove production-grade concurrency, real payment verification, database-backed audit trails, mobile-native push flows, or fraud detection. Those are production infrastructure questions. The current proof point is narrower and more important for this repo: messy shared-cost context becomes structured state, and deterministic code guards money-critical transitions.
